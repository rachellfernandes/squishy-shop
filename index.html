Hi, We’re Squishy Daze!

Squishy Daze is a small student-run business that offers big, soft, and slow-rising squishies in cute and fun designs. We aim to provide affordable products that students and other customers can enjoy during their free time.

Our squishies are perfect for squeezing, collecting, or giving as gifts. Whether you’re taking a break from schoolwork or simply looking for something fun, Squishy Daze brings a little comfort and enjoyment to your day.

  • Affordable and Student-Friendly. We offer products at reasonable prices.
  • Cute and Fun Designs. Choose from different shapes, colors, and characters.
  • Easy to Order. Customers can purchase through our online platform or directly at school.
